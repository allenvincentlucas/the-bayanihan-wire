Dispatch No. 1 Sport · Tennis

🎾Eala Claims Her First WTA 500 Crown in Washington

Filipina tennis phenom Alex Eala made history at the Mubadala Citi DC Open, rallying past world No. 3 Jessica Pegula of the United States 4-6, 6-4, 6-0 to win her first-ever WTA 500 singles title.

Eala is the first Filipino player ever to capture a WTA 500 crown. President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. sent a congratulatory message, calling her dedication an inspiration to young Filipinos nationwide.

Dispatch No. 2 Health · Regional Cooperation

🏥DOH Hosts ASEAN Conference on Noncommunicable Diseases

The Department of Health opened the ASEAN Conference on Noncommunicable Diseases 2026 in Manila as part of the Philippines' ASEAN Chairship this year. With 600 delegates and virtual participants from across Southeast Asia, member states are adopting a regional call to action for 2026–2030 to strengthen primary healthcare systems and coordinate against chronic conditions.

Dispatch No. 3 Community · Inclusion

♿Carmona Opens the Country's First Fully-Integrated PWD Facility

The City of Carmona launched the Philippines' first fully-integrated resource and residential facility for Persons with Disabilities, offering specialized medical care, vocational skills training, and residential support aimed at fostering independence within the PWD community.

Dispatch No. 4 Sport · Softball

🥎Sagay City Girls Win 2026 PONY World Series Title

The 12-Under Girls softball team from Sagay City, Negros Occidental (Jose B. Puey Sr. Elementary) brought home the championship at the 2026 PONY International West Zone Softball World Series in Hemet, California, besting more than ten squads — Sagay City's second international PONY World Series title this year, following their 14-Under win last month.

Dispatch No. 5 Community · Welfare

⚓Government Welcomes Home Injured Seafarers From the Black Sea

The Department of Migrant Workers and the Overseas Workers Welfare Administration welcomed home two Filipino seafarers injured in a Black Sea drone attack, extending full medical care, financial assistance, and psychological debriefing support as the pair begin their post-arrival recovery.
