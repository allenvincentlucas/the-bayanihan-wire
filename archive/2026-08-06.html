Dispatch No. 1 Sport · Tennis

🎾Eala Advances to Canadian Open Third Round

Fresh off her historic Washington DC Open title, Filipina tennis prodigy Alex Eala kept her winning momentum going, defeating Alycia Parks of the United States 6-1, 4-6, 6-2 to reach the third round of the 2026 Canadian Open in Toronto. She'll face Caty McNally next in a high-stakes matchup.

Dispatch No. 2 Community · Disaster Recovery

🏡Safe Return Home for Displaced Mayon Evacuees

Hundreds of displaced families living outside Mayon Volcano's 6-kilometer permanent danger zone were cleared by disaster risk management authorities and the DSWD to safely return home, receiving transition food packages, hygiene kits, and local livelihood recovery assistance as volcanic activity stabilizes.

Dispatch No. 3 Sport · Grassroots Development

🏋️PSC Invests ₱10 Million in Grassroots Sports in Antique

The Philippine Sports Commission turned over ₱10 million worth of high-grade athletic equipment, training gear, and sports supplies to public schools and local government units across Antique, aiming to strengthen regional sports academies and nurture young provincial athletes for national competition.

Dispatch No. 4 Policy · Housing Rights

⚖️Senate Exempts Poor Families From Costly Eviction Appeal Bonds

The Senate passed key amendments to the Urban Development and Housing Act exempting underprivileged and homeless citizens from posting expensive supersedeas bonds when appealing housing eviction cases, removing a major financial barrier for indigent families seeking legal review.

Dispatch No. 5 Policy · Education

📚New Law Gives DepEd More Flexibility to Boost Literacy

Newly lapsed legislation grants the Department of Education expanded flexibility in instructional methods and reading intervention programs, aiming to accelerate foundational literacy and numeracy nationwide — building on ARAL, the agency's tutoring program that helped 4.5 million of its 6.7 million identified struggling learners improve their reading this past school year.
